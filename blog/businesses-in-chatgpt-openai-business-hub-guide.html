Businesses in ChatGPT: How to Register, Verify & Showcase Your Catalog

November 2026 14 min read Official OpenAI Announcement
Share this executive briefing:

In 2014, Google launched Google My Business and permanently redefined local search. In November 2026, OpenAI launched "Businesses in ChatGPT" — and the era of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) officially began.

With over 250 million weekly active users asking ChatGPT for product recommendations, vendor comparisons, and local services, claiming and verifying your business profile is no longer optional — it is your company's front door to conversational AI.

Every week, millions of buyers bypass Google entirely. Instead of clicking through ten blue links and wrestling with keyword-stuffed SEO blogs, they open ChatGPT and ask: "Who is the best custom software company in India for SaaS architecture?", "Where can I buy ergonomic office chairs under $400 with fast shipping?", or "Compare the top WhatsApp API gateway platforms for Shopify."

Until now, ChatGPT had to guess what your company offered by crawling public web pages. That often led to outdated pricing, hallucinated specs, or omitted product catalogs. With Businesses in ChatGPT, OpenAI has introduced an official, authenticated Business Hub where companies can verify their legal identity, upload structured product feeds, deploy customer-facing business agents, and analyze conversational brand mentions.

At VitableTech, we have actively tested this platform during its closed rollout. In this comprehensive guide, we'll explain how the Business Hub works, how verification protects your brand, how product feeds power ChatGPT recommendations, and how our engineering team can help your company onboard seamlessly.

1. What is Businesses in ChatGPT? The 4 Core Pillars

OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT platform is designed to connect enterprises, e-commerce retailers, and local service providers directly to ChatGPT's conversational search graph. The system consists of four primary components:

1. Verified Business Profile

An authenticated entity card featuring official website, contact info, operating hours, and a verified checkmark backed by government ID verification.

2. Product Catalog Feeds

Direct file uploads (CSV/JSON) or Shopify store synchronization that supply ChatGPT with live pricing, stock availability, and checkout links.

3. Custom Business Agent

A branded conversational concierge that lives on your profile to answer prospective customer inquiries according to custom guidelines.

4. Brand Insights & Analytics

7-day and 28-day performance tracking measuring brand mentions, citation volume, user click-throughs, and conversation discovery topics.

2. Inside the Business Hub Dashboard: A Detailed Walkthrough

As captured in our live setup for vitabletech.in, the Business Hub replaces third-party guesswork with a clean, dark-mode control room:

2.1 The Identity Verification Barrier

"Verify your identity with a government-issued ID"

OpenAI enforces strict KYC (Know Your Customer) rules. Before you can edit your public business description or publish your customer-facing AI agent, an authorized company representative must submit a passport, national identity card, or driver's license. This completely blocks bad actors from claiming your brand name or running deceptive phishing agents.

2.2 "Get Ready to Chat with Customers" (Business Agent)

Unlike standard custom GPTs in the public GPT Store, a Business Agent is tied directly to your verified commercial domain. You configure specific guardrails, return policies, warranty terms, and conversational tone. When a user navigates to your business profile in ChatGPT, they can chat with your agent to resolve pre-sale objections.

2.3 "Upload Your Product Catalog"

This is where conversational commerce happens. By clicking into the catalog manager (/products?add=1), you can upload structured SKU listings. When prospective buyers ask ChatGPT questions like "What services does VitableTech provide for mobile app development?" or "Show me winter jackets with gore-tex under $200", ChatGPT can pull directly from your verified feed instead of hallucinating outdated third-party reviews.

3. Step-by-Step: How to Register & Verify Your Company

Navigating identity verification, DNS authentication, and catalog ingestion can be intricate. Here is the operational workflow:

  1. Access the Business Hub: Log in to your ChatGPT workspace account. Navigate to the sidebar and select the new Business menu option.
  2. Claim or Search for Your Business: Search for your registered business name or website domain (e.g., vitabletech.in). If a preliminary profile already exists from web crawl data, click Claim Business.
  3. Submit Government ID for Identity Verification: A designated director, officer, or legal owner must complete biometric or document verification using a government-issued ID (Passport, Driving License, or National ID).
  4. Domain & DNS Verification: Add a unique TXT record to your domain's DNS manager (Cloudflare, GoDaddy, AWS Route 53) to prove ownership of the corporate domain.
  5. Configure Business Info & Policies: Upload official brand assets (high-res SVG logo, cover banner, customer service email, corporate address, tax identification numbers, and terms of service).
💡 Important Verification Tip:

Ensure the legal entity name on your government-issued ID matches the registered domain registrant or corporate incorporation documents. Mismatches can delay the OpenAI review queue by several business days.

4. Product Catalogs: Turning ChatGPT into a High-Converting Sales Engine

Traditional search engines display static shopping carousels. ChatGPT operates differently: it engages in a two-way consultative dialogue. When your product catalog is properly formatted and uploaded, ChatGPT becomes your company's most knowledgeable product specialist.

Key Catalog Attributes Required

Attribute Field Description Why ChatGPT Needs It
sku / id Unique alphanumeric product identifier Prevents product collisions and duplicate mentions
title & description Detailed product name and technical specifications Allows semantic vector matching when users search by use-case
price & currency Live retail price with ISO currency code (USD, INR, EUR) ChatGPT cites exact pricing instead of guessing
availability in_stock, out_of_stock, or preorder Stops users from being directed to unavailable items
link / checkout_url Direct canonical landing page with UTM tracking Routes the user to complete checkout on your website

Whether you operate a Shopify DTC brand selling apparel or a B2B enterprise offering cloud software packages, having your catalog directly ingested into the Business Hub ensures that whenever a relevant user query arises, your verified products are showcased accurately.

5. Brand Insights: Welcome to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Just as Google Search Console provided webmasters with impressions and clicks, ChatGPT's Brand Insights tab provides the first official measurement of conversational AI visibility:

Mentions (7-Day & 28-Day Tracking)

Tracks the aggregate count of times ChatGPT referenced your company or included a direct citation link to your domain in user conversations. This is the new "organic impression" metric of the generative AI era.

Website Clicks

Directly counts users who clicked outbound citation links from ChatGPT answers to visit your web pages, pricing sheets, or catalog items.

Discovery Topics

Aggregated high-level themes showing which categories of user prompts triggered recommendations for your business — highlighting gaps where your content or catalog could expand.

⚠️ Privacy By Design

OpenAI explicitly guarantees that businesses cannot read individual user chat logs, prompts, or personal identifying data. All Brand Insights are strictly aggregated and anonymized over 7-day and 28-day windows to protect consumer privacy while still delivering actionable commercial intelligence.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Businesses in ChatGPT free to use?

Yes, claiming your profile, submitting identity verification, uploading your product catalog, and accessing brand insights are available without paying advertising fees during the current beta rollout.

Does verification guarantee my company will always rank #1 in ChatGPT answers?

No. OpenAI has made it clear that claiming a profile and supplying catalog data ensures your information is accurate and eligible for citation, but it does not buy or guarantee placement. Consistent technical SEO, clear product specs, positive web sentiment, and proper GEO practices drive recommendations.

How is a Business Agent different from a Custom GPT in the GPT Store?

A Custom GPT is an unverified user-created tool hosted in the general marketplace. A Business Agent is officially linked to an authenticated domain and verified corporate entity, appearing directly on your verified ChatGPT business profile with verified checkmarks.

What types of businesses are eligible?

Profile claiming, verification, and brand insights are available globally wherever ChatGPT is supported. Business Agents and interactive customer chat features are initially rolling out to eligible e-commerce and online service businesses with active websites.
